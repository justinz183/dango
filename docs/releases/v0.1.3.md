## What's new in 0.1.3

- **A new card for your unrevealed ABTI.** Until you have seen your ABTI, the panel shows a fan of cards with a question mark and four "?" for the four letters; "Reveal →" plays the story page by page.
- **The invitation shows wherever you are.** It now appears on every desktop and over full-screen apps, so it is no longer missed when you are working in a full-screen terminal. If the result isn't ready yet, it tries again a little later.
- **Quieter first run.** No more "reading your history" line after installing, and the little capsule above the dango stays still instead of bobbing.
