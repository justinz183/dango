## What's new in 0.1.1

- **Your ABTI, the first time you open DANGO.** Once your history has been read, a small window invites you to see your ABTI; open it and the story plays page by page, from the scan of your history to your card. "Later" asks again next time; it's always in the right-click menu.
- **Share the week or the month.** The panel's share button follows the tab on top: 1 day shares today's report, 7 days this week's, 30 days this month's. Right-click it to copy that report instead. On Windows the same button copies that report's text.
