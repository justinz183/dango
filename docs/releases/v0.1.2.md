## What's new in 0.1.2

- **Fixed: DANGO quit as soon as it opened on a Mac that never had it.** On a first install there was no `~/.dango` folder yet, and the app stopped before it could make one, so nothing appeared: no dango, no counting, no ABTI. If you installed 0.1.0 or 0.1.1 and saw nothing, install again and it opens.
- **Your ABTI waits for you.** Until you have seen your ABTI story, the card in the panel doesn't give the result away: "See my ABTI →" plays it page by page, from the scan of your history to your card.
- If you already saw the story in 0.1.0, you are invited once more to see it the new way.
